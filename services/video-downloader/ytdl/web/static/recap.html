Thumbnail
Facebook, TikTok, Instagram and YouTube all ignore a file’s cover art — their cover pickers only offer frames from the video. Putting the thumbnail on the front makes it one of those frames, so it can be chosen on a phone and is what the profile grid falls back to. Viewers see it, so keep it short.
Nothing here is rendered until you ask. Regenerating a line, changing the mix or re-cutting all need another render — but never another round of speech.
